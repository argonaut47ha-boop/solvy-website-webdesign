FAQ

Straight answers.

Is there a contract?

$0-down plans run 12 months, then go month to month with no fees. If you pay the $499 setup on Foundation, you stay month to month from day one. Either way you get a 30-day trial after launch.

Who owns my website?

You own your content, domain, and brand. A published buyout schedule lets you take the custom code with you if you leave. [Edit to match your final terms.]

Do you guarantee rankings or AI citations?

No. No one can honestly promise that. We guarantee the setup, the optimization work, and clear monthly reporting.

What's the difference between SEO and AEO?

SEO helps you rank in Google. AEO structures your answers so voice assistants, snippets, and AI tools can understand and quote your business.

What is the knowledge assistant?

A private AI for your staff, trained on your own documents: price lists, procedures, FAQs. It's separate from the public website chatbot. See our AI policy.

Why custom instead of WordPress or a template?

Custom builds ship less code, load faster, and can be tuned exactly to your business, with no plugin stack to maintain.

How long does it take?

Usually under a week to a first draft. It goes live as soon as you approve it.
